Hi! My name is Anton Potapov and I'm a Game Designer.

I've been on this journey for a while and have had different experience in many areas of gamedev industry. I adhere to Systemic Design principles as they are laid out in books by Tracey Fullerton, Ernest Adams, Brenda Romero and Geoffrey Engelstein. I don't care about the genre and love the broad spectrum of fun whether it's videogames, boardgames, wargames, TTRPGs, math games, puzzles or sports.

Check my Portfolio in Notion to get acquainted with the list of mechanics I helped to implement and projects I've worked on!

You can check my personal Telegram channel dedicated to games of all kinds. There are also some videos with my appearance: an UFL Journey vlog and a public discussion on Systemic Game Design.

Aside from games of all kinds and origins I'm very interested in history, law, linguistics, arts, Fender Telecaster guitars and STEM.
